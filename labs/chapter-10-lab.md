# Chapter 10 Lab: Bayesian Reasoning, Linear Algebra, and Calculus Foundations

**Estimated time:** 35 minutes

## Exercise

Implement Bayesian updating for an A/B test using conjugate priors, compute an eigen decomposition for PCA by hand and verify it with numpy, and test time-series stationarity with the Augmented Dickey-Fuller test

## Key takeaway

Bayesian reasoning, linear algebra, and calculus are the mathematical backbone of PCA, optimization, and time-series methods tested throughout the exam.
