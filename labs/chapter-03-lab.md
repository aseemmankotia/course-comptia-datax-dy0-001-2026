# Chapter 3 Lab: Model Evaluation, Iteration, and Communicating Outcomes

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Compute ROC-AUC and PR-AUC on an imbalanced fraud dataset, plot a calibration curve, and draft a one-page stakeholder summary translating the metrics into business impact

## Key takeaway

Choose evaluation metrics that match the class distribution and business goal, then translate them into concrete stakeholder language, not just numbers.
