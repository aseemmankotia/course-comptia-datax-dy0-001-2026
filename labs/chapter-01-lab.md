# Chapter 1 Lab: Exploratory Data Analysis and Data Quality Triage

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Using pandas and imbalanced-learn on a churn dataset: detect a target-leakage feature, quantify a missingness pattern, then apply SMOTE only after the train/test split

## Key takeaway

Every EDA step must be checked for leakage and correct missingness assumptions before any imputation, scaling, or resampling occurs.
